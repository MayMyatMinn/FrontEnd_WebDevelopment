About Us

At Bean Boutique Coffee Shop, we are passionate about coffee and dedicated to creating a unique experience for every customer. Founded by a dynamic family with a love for both tradition and innovation, we bring together the finest coffee beans from around the world to craft perfect blends that will captivate your senses. Our mission is to share the rich history and diverse flavors of coffee with every visitor, whether you're a casual coffee lover or a connoisseur. Join us on a journey of discovery, where each sip tells a story of excellence, quality, and the artistry of coffee making.

Founder Story

Founded by U Soe and Daw Thiri, Bean Boutique Coffee Shop is built on the foundation of a deep love for coffee and a shared desire to introduce the world to its rich flavors and aroma. Together, they have created a warm and welcoming space for coffee lovers, forging a community that continues to grow with every passing day. Their daughter, May Myat Minn, has been a driving force behind the shop's success as its CEO since 2020. A globally recognized IT professional, May Myat Minn has blended her passion for technology with her family's love for coffee, helping to elevate Bean Boutique to new heights. With a commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainability, the family’s vision continues to inspire customers and shape the future of coffee culture.

About Our CEO

May Myat Minn, the CEO of Bean Boutique Coffee Shop since 2020, has played a pivotal role in shaping the future of our brand. A globally recognized IT professional, May is known for her innovative approach to blending technology with traditional business. With a deep passion for both coffee and technology, she has led Bean Boutique into a new era, introducing cutting-edge digital solutions that enhance our customer experience and streamline our operations. Her dedication to sustainability, creativity, and excellence has earned her widespread recognition in the IT world, while her leadership continues to inspire the entire Bean Boutique family. Under her guidance, we strive to be at the forefront of innovation, offering not only the finest coffee but also a tech-savvy and customer-centric experience.

About Our Team

At Bean Boutique Coffee Shop, our success is driven by a passionate and dedicated team that shares a love for coffee and a commitment to exceptional service. Each member of our team plays a vital role in delivering an unforgettable experience for our customers. From our skilled baristas who expertly craft each cup to our behind-the-scenes team ensuring smooth operations, everyone contributes to creating the warm, welcoming atmosphere that defines Bean Boutique. Together, we embrace the values of quality, creativity, and collaboration. Our diverse team brings unique perspectives and talents to the table, making us a close-knit family focused on providing outstanding coffee and customer service every day. We continue to grow, learn, and innovate, all while staying true to our mission of sharing the joy of coffee with the world.

What is Bean Boutique Coffee Shop?

Bean Boutique Coffee Shop is more than just a coffee shop. It is a unique destination for coffee enthusiasts who value high-quality coffee products. We provide premium coffee beans, coffee machines, and coffee powder packs to cater to both casual coffee drinkers and connoisseurs. Whether you're looking for a quick caffeine fix or want to explore unique flavors, our shop has everything you need to make your coffee experience extraordinary.

Can I order online?

Absolutely! We offer a user-friendly online shopping experience where you can browse and purchase our products at your convenience. Once you place your order, you can choose between home delivery or store pickup, whichever suits you best. Our secure payment methods and fast delivery ensure that your coffee essentials reach you in no time, so you can enjoy your favorite brews without stepping out.

Do you offer subscriptions?

Yes, we do! Our subscription plans are perfect for coffee lovers who want a consistent supply of fresh, high-quality coffee beans delivered directly to their doorsteps. You can choose from our monthly, bi-weekly, or even weekly subscriptions to ensure that you never run out of coffee. Subscribers also get exclusive access to limited-edition flavors and special discounts on our products.

What types of coffee beans do you sell?

We sell a diverse range of coffee beans, including Arabica, Robusta, Liberica, and Excelsa. Each type has its own unique flavor profile, catering to different tastes and preferences. From smooth and mild Arabica beans to bold and intense Robusta, we aim to provide a wide selection for every coffee enthusiast. Explore our collection to find your perfect cup!

Do you provide brewing equipment?

Yes, we provide a wide range of brewing equipment to suit all your coffee-making needs. From state-of-the-art coffee machines to classic French presses, grinders, filters, and scoops, our collection ensures that you can brew coffee just the way you like it. Our staff is always happy to assist you in selecting the right equipment based on your preferences and budget.
